DEVELOPER & SOUND DESIGNER

Hiam Louis "Yam" Bartolome is a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng Valenzuela (PLV) in Valenzuela City, Metro Manila. Specializing in full-stack architecture, relational database engineering, and gameplay mechanics, Yam combines computational problem-solving with artistic audio production.

Beyond software engineering, Yam is the multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, and audio producer behind the musical moniker Aislefront. By blending dynamic audio synthesis, diverse soundscapes, and expressive melodic scoring directly with game loops and interactive software, he builds experiences that resonate both mechanically and emotionally.
